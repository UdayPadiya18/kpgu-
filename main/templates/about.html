Welcome To Kiran & Pallavi Global University

About KPGU...

Established as Private University in 2021 under the provisions of:

Gujarat Private Universities Act, 2009 and Gujarat Private Universities (Amendment) Act, 2021 (Gujarat Act 15 of 2021)

Notification of Education Department, Government of Gujarat

Drs. Kiran & Pallavi Patel Global University (KPGU) Vadodara, (formerly BITS Edu Campus) offers quality education in Engineering and Technology, Health-Care, Science, Commerce and Management, and Nursing.

  • KPGU aims to become a "University of Global Eminence," aligned with its vision to serve higher education, healthcare, and humanity with relentless pursuit of progress. The university’s transformation from an affiliating institution into a self-governing university marks a key milestone in its journey toward this goal. This success is possible due to dedicated efforts of the entire "Team Bits Edu Campus," under the guidance of Shree Krishna Educational & Charitable Trust.
  • By adhering to the KPGU Vision-2040, KPGU has positioned itself as a "Gateway to Global Excellence," to explore opportunities that will elevate performance to new levels. This growth reflects the university’s mission of globalizing knowledge through creation, acquisition, preservation, and exchange, and fostering a collaborative environment that nurtures innovative, compassionate, and socially responsible global citizens.
  • KPGU is dedicated to achieving multifaceted excellence across academics, research, collaboration, outreach activities, skill development, and employability initiatives. These efforts ensure the continual progress of all stakeholders and reinforce KPGU's commitment to shaping individuals with scientific temper, empathy, courage, and creativity, contributing meaningfully to society.
  • With this transformational framework, KPGU is prepared to achieve national and international recognition, advancing the cause of higher education, healthcare, and service to mankind, while remaining ever-focused on new horizons and continual growth.
  • The vision of our esteemed Founder President, Shree Jagdishbhai D. Patel, to establish KPGU Vadodara as Gujarat's first foreign university campus is swiftly becoming a reality. Guided by global standards and benchmarks, this initiative is designed to secure a bright future for our students. This landmark achievement aligns with the provisions of the New Education Policy (NEP-2020).
  • Globally acclaimed University from the USA is associating with KPGU, Vadodara as Gujarat Campus through efforts and patronage of Dr. Kiran C. Patel, a proponent of Indo-American initiatives from Florida, USA.
  • KPGU has increased infrastructure to about 18 lakhs Sq.ft. and focused on Skill, Interdisciplinary, Industry driven curriculum with global appeal, collaborative research, social inclusiveness w.r.t. National Development Goals through its qualified faculty.
