Message from Director

Welcome to the esteemed Krishna School of Diploma Studies (KSDS) at Drs. Kiran & Pallavi Patel Global University (KPGU), Vadodara. As the Director, I am honoured to lead our dedicated team in fostering academic excellence and innovation.
